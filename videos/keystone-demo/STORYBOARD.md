# Keystone demo film — plan (dispatch artifact)

Built inline, one pass. `flow: automation`, `storyboard: no`. Each frame below is an act; `src` is the composition the builder writes from `scripts/acts/<id>.mjs`. Durations are driven by the measured narration in `narration.json` (lead + VO + beats + tail), so the timecodes here are approximate and the builder's output (`timings.json`) is the truth.

Transition vocabulary (hyperframes-animation/transitions): **push** is the primary (related points); **zoom-through** opens the loop, the callback and the strongest act; **blur crossfade** where the picture continues or the register calms; **dip** where the register changes; one **whip** for the relay. Every act has an entrance, a focal moment, and hands off through the transition (no exit fades except the last scene).

Audio identity: "Beauty Flow" (Kevin MacLeod, CC BY 4.0) as the bed, carved under the narration group; bundled SFX marks (impact-bass on the stamp and the gate, pings on the marker circles, pops on chips, whooshes on the relay, chime on the lift, sparkle on the split); the bed ducks hard on "and here it stops", lifts on the turn, drops under the build reflection, and fades under the credit card.
