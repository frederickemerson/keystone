Keystone

Spoken judgement becomes a governed Intelligence Pill

Solution diagram · Keppel AI HARVEST
Pill in focus: Technical Services, chilled-water leak · fictional data
1

Business problem

Incident-response judgement sits with a few senior engineers, is applied inconsistently and leaves when they retire. A wrong call on a leak means unsafe re-energisation, tenant/SLA exposure and downtime. A chatbot can't be trusted to act.

2

Capture what was never written

Whose: Tan Wei Ming, retiring Chief Engineer, 18 years of M&E.

  • Onboarding call with CDM + ACTA probes (simulated in the demo): "walk me through the last time…"
  • Contradiction resolution: "always isolate the riser" vs "isolate the sub-valve" becomes one condition (TS-02)
  • Expert confirms, edits or rejects every rule on the Decision Canvas
3

Compile into a reusable pill

A plain-text package the organisation owns:

SKILL.mdrules.yamlevidence.yamlprovenance.jsonevals/CHANGELOG.md
  • 13 rules, each with action class, owner, deadline and expert quote + timestamp
  • Owner, steward and approver named per pill
  • Runs as a native CodeBuddy skill (verified): same TS-07 decision
4

Asset Operations Manager supervises

Incident Room: a deterministic engine applies the pill to evidence.

RecommendRequires approvalMust escalateBlocked
  • Verify: Why drawer shows rule, version, missing evidence and the expert's quote, read aloud
  • Escalate: TS-07 blocks re-energisation and routes to the duty LEW by name
  • Approve: named humans only; server-side role-based redaction
5

More than a chatbot

  • Deterministic gates an LLM cannot talk past
  • Provenance to the spoken sentence
  • Evals replay past incidents
  • Governance: versions, owners, audit, rollback
  • Engine takes no model input: an LLM change can't alter a gate
6
