# Keystone — demo video v2 (shooting script)

**Target 3:05.** Narrated by one voice. Upbeat, fast cuts, motion graphics over live
product. Text on screen is a *punctuation mark*, never a paragraph — if the narrator says
it, the screen does not spell it out.

**Why it is structured this way:** it opens on the failure, not on us. The exact question
asked in the cold open is asked again at 2:28 and answered correctly. That callback is the
whole argument, and it is the thing the current cut does not have.

---

## Voice direction

Conversational, quick, a bit wry. You are showing a mate something you are pleased with —
not presenting to a board. Short sentences. Land the full stops. Where a line is marked
**[beat]**, stop properly: the picture is doing the work.

Do **not** read the bracketed stage directions.

## Production notes

- **Total VO ≈ 420 words.** At a natural pace that's ~3:00 with the held beats.
- **Text on screen total: under 25 words.** If a card is explaining, cut it — the voice
  explains, the picture proves.
- **Never show a static screen for more than 3 seconds** without motion — push-in, parallax,
  or a graphic landing. This is the single biggest fix from v1.
- **The cold open and Act 5 must be frame-identical** in framing. The callback only works if
  the viewer recognises the shot.
- **Music:** keep "Beauty Flow" (CC BY 4.0, credit card already cut) *or* swap to something
  with more drive — if swapped, the credit card changes with it and the licence must be
  re-checked before use.
- **Everything claimed here is true and on-screen-able:** the 2.9s, the 5 passages, the
  invented "v2.1", the contradiction timestamps, 4/3/6 recurrence, `distance_to_electrical_m`.
  Nothing in this script needs a mock-up.
