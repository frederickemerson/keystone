# Keystone — red-team judge scorecard

Date: 2026-10-12 (4 days to the 16 Oct submission). Reviewer stance: a tough Keppel first-round judge (11 "should be" properties, 8 diagram points) plus a Tencent Cloud judge (use of Tencent AI tools, feasibility). Read-only review; no code was changed.

**Evidence base.** Handbook §3 and §11, `docs/research/research_keppel_critique.md`, `docs/submission/*`, `README.md`, `docs/DECISIONS.md`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/SUBMISSION.md` §7–13, the pill files, and a full walk of the live app at https://keystone-tencent.vercel.app via Playwright as Tan Wei Ming, Priya Nair, Goh Boon Kheng and Rajesh Kumar (26 screenshots in `.playwright-mcp/judge-*.jpeg`, gitignored). `npx vitest run` was executed locally: **33 files, 515 tests, all passing** (docs still say 32 / 500).

**Bottom line.** The product is real and the governance loop works end to end on the live URL (escalate → propose → shadow 6/6 → separate approver → rollback, with server-side redaction verified in the served HTML). That is better than most entries will be. What can lose it: (1) no Tencent model runs anywhere on the live demo, and the one live AI button calls OpenAI, takes ~60 s and then falls back to a fixture; (2) several claims a judge can falsify in one click ("his own voice", "every tile carries the quote", "model swap gives the same decision", test counts); (3) the video is not recorded and the repo is private.

## 4. Overclaims and inaccuracies a judge can catch (fix every one)

1. **"That's Tan Wei Ming's own voice, at fourteen minutes thirty-two"** (`docs/submission/video-script.md:76`, also "clip" at `README.md:51` and script line 69). It is `window.speechSynthesis` reading the quote (`components/incident/useExpertVoice.ts`, `components/capture/useCallPlayback.ts`); there are no recordings in `public/`.
2. **"Every tile carries its transcript quote + timestamp"** (README step 2; script 2:05) — Canvas shows a placeholder sentence; see Transparent above.
3. **"Swap the model (hy4-preview → deepseek-v4-pro) and the decision is identical"** (`README.md:53`; diagram point 5; `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`; `docs/SUBMISSION.md` §8/§9). No control exists; the video script admits it.
4. **"32 test files, 500 tests"** (`video-script.md:14, 106, 109, 178`; form-answers §3) — now 33 / 515. Harmless but the video must not end on a stale number.
5. **"12 candidate tiles" / "All 12 rules confirmed"** (`README.md:48`, pill `CHANGELOG.md` 1.2.0) — the Canvas shows 13 (TS-13 was added in D4).
6. **"AI proposes (hy4-preview)"** in the Incident Room — a recorded fixture labelled with a model name and no "recorded" marker (`IncidentRoom.tsx:916`); everywhere else recorded output is badged.
7. **"Pills as CodeBuddy skills (CodeBuddy verified)"** — `docs/proof/04a` prompt: "Per this skill's rules.yaml, what is TS-07's status, who is escalated to, and what did the Chief Engineer say (provenance.json)?" 8. **"With no CodeBuddy key and no TokenHub key, the API selects recorded fixtures immediately"** (`README.md:208`) — true only if `OPENAI_API_KEY` is also unset; on Vercel it is set, so the public demo calls OpenAI and waits up to 50 s.

10. **Business-value figures** flagged `[verify before submitting]` in form-answers §4 — either verify or delete; a Keppel judge will know the escape-of-water numbers.

## 5. Three biggest risks of losing

**Risk 1 — The Tencent-stack story collapses on contact with the live URL.** Nothing Tencent runs live: no Hunyuan via CodeBuddy (no key on Vercel), TokenHub blocked by KYC, WorkBuddy/ADP untested, and the single live-AI control calls OpenAI, spins for ~60 s and falls back. The two live ML calls that do work are OpenAI and TypeSafe Jev. Tencent judges score "use of AI tools" and "feasibility" and own half the first-round panel. Proof-of-use screenshots clear the hard gate, but the product itself currently argues against the pitch.

**Risk 2 — One-click falsifiable claims erode trust in the governance claims that are actually true.** "Own voice", Canvas quote, model swap, fixture labelled as a live model, leading CodeBuddy prompt. Once a judge catches two of these, "500 tests, server-side redaction, deterministic gates" get discounted too, and Responsible AI (a dimension this entry should win) drops with them.

**Risk 3 — Submission hygiene and demo fragility.** Video not recorded and the landing page says "coming soon"; GitHub repo is private (`form-answers.md:181`); unverified figures; Incident Room overflows on phones (judges open links on phones); a 60-second dead button on the capture screen; governance state in `localStorage` means a judge who shares a laptop sees another judge's v1.4.0 rollback unless they press "Reset demo". Half-finished edges read as incompetence, not ambition (critique §B1).
