## Facts measured during the audit

- `npx vitest run`: **33 test files, 514 passed, 1 skipped (515 tests)**.
- Provider chain (`lib/llm/providers.ts:25-31`): CodeBuddy Agent SDK runs Hunyuan `hy4-preview` with a 25 s timeout. If that fails it tries TokenHub (15 s), then OpenAI `gpt-5.4-mini` (20 s; `lib/llm/client.ts:54`), then the recorded fixture. The whole chain has a 45 s budget. The CodeBuddy local CLI login is switched off on Vercel (`providers.ts:39`).
- Vercel Production environment variables (`vercel env ls`, run separately by both reviewers): `OPENAI_API_KEY`, `JEV_API_KEY` and `KEYSTONE_SESSION_SECRET`. There is no `CODEBUDDY_API_KEY` and no `TOKENHUB_API_KEY`. **So on the deployed site, live AI comes from OpenAI only, or from the recorded fixture. Hunyuan never answers there.**
- Proof screenshots:
  - `docs/proof/01a`: CodeBuddy in Plan mode on "Hy4 preview", with sub-agents ("Agent x2").
  - `docs/proof/04a` and `04b`: the `/chilled-water-leak` skill is invoked from `.codebuddy/skills` and gives the TS-07 BLOCKED decision. The model selector shows **"Auto"**, not hy4-preview.
- `.codebuddy/` in git: only `skills/` is tracked, and it is byte-identical to `pills/`. No plans are committed.
- "Hear the expert" uses browser text-to-speech (`components/incident/useExpertVoice.ts`, `components/capture/useCallPlayback.ts`). The repo contains no audio files.
- The incident timeline (`data/timelines/INC-2026-1011.json`) has 17 events. Event 10 is at 08:11:00, which is 8 min 42 s after event 1 (08:02:18). So "T+08:42" is correct, but it reads like a clock time; "T+8m42s" would be clearer.

## Summary

There are 137 merged claims: **28 FALSE, 47 OVERSTATED, 12 UNVERIFIABLE, 50 TRUE**. 53 claims were flagged by both reviewers; the rest were caught by only one. The "Reviewers" column shows which.

**Highest-priority fixes:**

1. **Provider attribution.**
   - Several places describe the AI as Hunyuan only, or say Vercel shows recorded output with no network call: `README.md:207-211,238-240`; `docs/SUBMISSION.md:157,201-215,430-437`; `form-answers.md:83`; `solution-diagram.html:196,200`; `ARCHITECTURE.md:120-122`.
   - In fact OpenAI answers live on the deployed site, and `SUBMISSION.md:201-206` contradicts the correct chain given in `README.md:193-202`.
2. **Stale test count.** `form-answers.md:95` and `video-script.md:14,106,109,152,178` say "500 tests, 32 files". The measured count is 515 tests (514 passed, 1 skipped) in 33 files.
3. **"Recorded clip" / "his own voice".** "Hear the expert" is described as a recording, but it is browser text-to-speech.
4. **Features claimed as visible in the product that do not exist:**
   - deny-by-default for unknown actions
   - the override-rate KPI
   - the incident-report pipeline
   - the AI Verify mapping
   - enforcement of the `access` frontmatter
   - an "immutable" audit log (it is in localStorage)
5. **Wrong numbers:**
   - "4/4 agreements": the app shows 6/6.
   - "12 tiles": there are 13.
   - "Seven routes, `/` = Pulse": `/` is the landing page and Pulse is at `/pulse`.
   - "9-word blurb": it has 8 words.
   - "Sat 11 – Wed 15 Oct": 11 Oct 2026 is a Sunday and 15 Oct a Thursday.
   - "moisture < X": a leftover placeholder.
   - "Node 18.20.8+": the dependencies need Node 22+.
6. **Contradictions with DECISIONS.md:**
   - `README.md:98-101` says the role lives in localStorage and redaction happens on the client. Decision D1 says the role comes from a server session and redaction runs on the server.
   - The approver in the video script and solution diagram does not match D2.
7. **Tencent integrations.**
   - TRTC, EdgeOne, WorkBuddy and ADP are correctly marked as proposed in `ARCHITECTURE.md` and `README.md`.
   - But `pills/README.md:61-72` and `SUBMISSION.md:366-369` describe them in the present tense.
   - TokenHub has never answered live (KYC is still pending).
   - The only Tencent pieces integrated today are the CodeBuddy skill and the CodeBuddy Agent SDK. The SDK runs locally only.
8. **Business-value figures marked [verify]** (US$89k average loss, 40% of PMETs aged 50+) are still in `form-answers.md`, which is labelled ready to paste. "Pays for the pilot" has no pilot cost behind it. All are UNVERIFIABLE.
9. **Fictional personas in SKILL.md.** Both SKILL.md persona bios mention the real "Keppel REIT asset portfolio". This contradicts the statement that all personnel are fictional.
